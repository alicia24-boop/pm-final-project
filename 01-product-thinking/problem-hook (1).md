# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Strategic crisis
Engagement continues to plateau, high-value users churn, and StreamLine becomes another undifferentiated content library competing on volume instead of quality.

## Moment of misery
Users are leaving the app to search Google, Reddit, reviews, and curated lists because browsing 15,000 titles makes it difficult to quickly find something worth watching.

## Problem hook
We must prevent engagement and retention from declining by helping overwhelmed users quickly discover high-quality content they actually want to watch.

## Value proposition
For Viewers overwhelmed by too many choices who want to find something worth watching quickly., we will Deliver curated, high-quality recommendations that make discovery faster and easier. because Engagement is plateauing and users may churn if StreamLine does not reduce choice overload and create a more differentiated experience..

## Cold-read self-review
Reading it cold, the strongest part is the clear risk of plateauing engagement and churn. The weakest part is proving that choice overload is widespread enough to justify a new initiative. I’ll sharpen it by tying the user pain more directly to retention and showing that this is a recurring problem for a meaningful segment of users.
